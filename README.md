# Hey, I'm Mohammad

[Website](https://farvisun.com) · [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/farvisun/) · [GitHub](https://github.com/farvisun)

I build backend platforms, AI-assisted products, and automation systems. My work usually sits at the intersection of production engineering, developer workflows, data systems, and practical AI.

Right now my main focus is AI: agents, LLM-powered workflows, internal tools, product copilots, and systems that turn messy context into useful actions. I care about AI that connects to real software, real teams, and real operating constraints.

## AI Focus

- AI agents that can plan, call tools, use context, and help teams execute work.
- LLM workflows for task management, knowledge retrieval, automation, and decision support.
- RAG-style systems with embeddings, search, vector databases, and structured context pipelines.
- Product copilots that sit inside real workflows instead of living as isolated chat boxes.
- Evaluation, observability, and feedback loops for AI features after they reach production.
- Practical prompt design, context engineering, and system design around model limitations.

## What I Work On

- Backend and platform architecture for high-traffic products.
- APIs, queues, caches, service migrations, and production scaling.
- Data, search, and analytics systems that make operations easier to understand.
- Internal tools that reduce repetitive work and improve team velocity.
- Engineering leadership: team design, ownership, roadmap planning, incident process, and delivery health.

## Current Interests

- Making AI tools useful inside everyday engineering and product workflows.
- Building agentic systems with clear boundaries, reliable actions, and measurable outcomes.
- Connecting LLMs to backend services, databases, queues, search, and internal knowledge.

- Designing team workflows where AI helps without creating hidden operational risk.

## How I Build

Make the critical path boring. Keep ownership clear. Add observability before panic. Use AI where it removes friction, exposes context, or helps people make better decisions.
